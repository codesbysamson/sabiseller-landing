Privacy Policy

How GateVend, currently operating as SabiSeller, handles information when businesses and their customers use our WhatsApp-powered commerce services.

Effective date: 17 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 17 September 2026

GateVend, currently operating under the SabiSeller name and domain, provides conversational commerce tools that help participating businesses respond to customer enquiries, share product information, capture orders, follow up on sales conversations, and manage related customer-service activity. References to “GateVend,” “SabiSeller,” “we,” or “our” in this policy refer to the same service. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you interact with GateVend/SabiSeller or a participating business using the service, we may process:

  • Contact information, such as your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Messages, enquiries, product interests, order details, delivery location, and other information you choose to provide during a conversation.
  • Business information supplied by participating merchants, including product catalogues, prices, FAQs, operating hours, delivery terms, and payment instructions.
  • Technical and operational information, such as message identifiers, timestamps, delivery status, webhook events, and service logs used for security, reliability, and troubleshooting.

Please do not send card PINs, passwords, authentication codes, or other unnecessary sensitive information through SabiSeller.

2. How we use information

  • To answer enquiries and provide requested product or business information.
  • To capture, confirm, update, or cancel orders and notify the relevant merchant.
  • To follow up on incomplete enquiries where appropriate.
  • To improve service quality, prevent duplicate processing, detect abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • To produce operational summaries and demand insights for the participating merchant.
  • To comply with applicable law and protect users, merchants, and our services.

3. How information is shared

Information may be shared with the business you are contacting because SabiSeller processes the conversation on that business’s behalf. We may also use service providers that support messaging, cloud hosting, databases, artificial-intelligence processing, monitoring, and communications. These providers process information only as necessary to deliver their services and are subject to their own contractual and privacy obligations.

WhatsApp messages are also processed by Meta and WhatsApp under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the requirements of the participating merchant. Information that is no longer required is deleted or anonymised where reasonably practicable.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No internet-based service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you. You may also stop messaging a participating business or block the business on WhatsApp. Certain information may be retained where required by law, needed for security, or necessary to establish or defend legal claims.

7. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your SabiSeller data:

  1. Email hello.cornerstonedigital@gmail.com with the subject “GateVend Data Deletion Request.”
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number used to contact the business and, if known, the name of that business.
  3. Do not include passwords, PINs, authentication codes, or payment-card details.

We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request. We will review verified requests and delete or anonymise eligible information, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and legitimate record-keeping requirements.

8. Children’s privacy

GateVend/SabiSeller is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the service, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, concerns, or data-deletion requests, email hello.cornerstonedigital@gmail.com.
